Welcome to Kinematics and Dynamics of Robots (25PC1RA204).

This course builds the mathematical foundation for describing and predicting the motion of
robot manipulators. It begins with the spatial description of rigid bodies — representing
points, frames and poses with rotation matrices and homogeneous transformations — and
develops forward kinematics for serial manipulators using the Denavit–Hartenberg convention.
It then covers inverse kinematics, differential motion and the manipulator Jacobian,
including singularity analysis, and closes with dynamics: deriving equations of motion
through Lagrangian mechanics and relating joint torques to end-effector forces through
static force analysis.
