Welcome to **Kinematics and Dynamics of Robots (25PC1RA204)**! The course is a mathematical
introduction to robot manipulators, from describing a single rigid body in space to modeling
the forces that drive a complete arm. Topics covered include **rigid-body transformations**
with rotation matrices and homogeneous transformations, **forward kinematics** using the
Denavit–Hartenberg convention, **inverse kinematics** of three- and six-degree-of-freedom
arms, **differential motion, the Jacobian and singularities**, and **dynamics and static force
analysis** with Lagrangian mechanics.

For Course Objectives, Course Outcomes, and Articulation Matrix, you can download the
official R&AI syllabus [here](https://www.vnrvjiet.ac.in/__l5e/assets-v1/2ffff3c9-daea-4ba1-bc7e-42dea9745c15/II-B.Tech-RAI-R25.pdf).
